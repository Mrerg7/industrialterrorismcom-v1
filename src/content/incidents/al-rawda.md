The November 2017 assault on the al-Rawda (Sufi) mosque killed approximately 305–311 people, including children. Perpetrators affiliated with the Islamic State chose the target partly because of doctrinal hostility toward Sufi practices. The attack combined an initial explosion with sustained small-arms fire.
