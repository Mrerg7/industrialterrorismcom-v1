The 2017 attack on the Dar al-Farooq Islamic Center in Bloomington, Minnesota involved both a pipe bomb and arson. It remains one of the more serious documented cases of ideologically motivated violence against a U.S. mosque in the last decade. Federal investigators classified the incident as a hate crime driven by anti-Muslim extremism.
